# Cooling Pump Overheat Runbook

## Symptoms

- Pump temperature is above threshold.
- Cooling flow is below normal.
- Vibration or abnormal noise increases.

## Checks

1. Confirm pump outlet and inlet valves are fully open.
2. Check whether the strainer or filter is blocked.
3. Compare motor current with the normal load range.
4. Inspect bearing temperature and vibration trend.
5. Confirm standby pump availability before any shutdown.

## Mitigation

1. Reduce load if process conditions allow.
2. Switch to standby pump if temperature or vibration keeps rising.
3. Clean the strainer after isolation and lockout.
4. Do not restart repeatedly if vibration remains above threshold.
