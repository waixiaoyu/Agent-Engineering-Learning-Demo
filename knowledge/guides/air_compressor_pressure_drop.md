# Air Compressor Pressure Drop Runbook

## Symptoms

- Discharge pressure below setpoint.
- Load and unload cycles become frequent.
- Filter differential pressure rises.

## Checks

1. Confirm downstream demand did not suddenly increase.
2. Check inlet filter differential pressure.
3. Inspect condensate drain and obvious air leaks.
4. Compare motor current with normal baseline.

## Mitigation

1. Switch to standby compressor if pressure continues falling.
2. Replace or clean the inlet filter if differential pressure is high.
3. Isolate major leak points.
4. Escalate if pressure cannot recover within the operating window.
